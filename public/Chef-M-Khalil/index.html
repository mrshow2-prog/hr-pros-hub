The Chef

A Culinary Vision Built on 19 Years of Excellence

Executive Sous Chef whose career has been forged across the finest 5-star resorts in the UAE, Oman, and Egypt. From the grand kitchens of Atlantis The Palm and Four Seasons to leading the pre-opening of Anantara World Islands Dubai, Chef Khalil brings a rare blend of technical mastery and visionary leadership.

Specializing in multi-cuisine execution — Arabic, Asian, Mediterranean, Steakhouse, and International — he is equally at home designing an intimate tasting menu as orchestrating a grand VIP banquet for hundreds of guests. Every plate is a statement of precision, seasonality, and artistry.

A builder of kitchens and people alike: he has reduced food costs by up to 20%, earned TripAdvisor's top restaurant recognition in Oman, won multiple Salon Culinaire medals, and led a team to a Guinness World Record achievement.
